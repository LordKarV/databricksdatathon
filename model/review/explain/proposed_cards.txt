Proposed card wording and layout (input for the explanation layer and the App)
==============================================================================

Source of every number below: cases.json, recompute_s1.json, audit_benchmark_cards.json in this folder, built from
the prototype tables. Facility names are real records from the dataset. Phone numbers are left out on purpose.

Layout rule 1. Three levels, the action comes first.
  Level 0, the phone view (at most 4 short lines, fits a basic phone screen or one SMS): what to do, where, what to
    ask on the call, and one plain line on how sure we are.
  Level 1, "Why this one" (one tap): quotes, what we ignored, where the location comes from, why it ranks above a
    nearer option.
  Level 2, "How the score was made" (judge view): the waterfall in points, with the calibration slope shown.

Layout rule 2. One badge per card, from this fixed list. Hard warnings come before the name.
  [CHECKED BY US]   reserved for a future verified list (X family). Not used in v1.
  [LIKELY]          the band rule holds. If P_S is within 0.05 of t_L, add "(borderline)".
  [CALL FIRST]      Unverified with at least one own sentence about the service (T >= 2).
  [NO SERVICE TEXT] Unverified with no own sentence (T < 2). Shown under a fold, never as the first option for an
                    emergency (see rule 4).
  [PLACE UNCLEAR]   P_L < 0.7, any location vote is -1, or the stated city could not be placed on the map. Shown
                    before the name, in the strongest colour on the page.

Layout rule 3. "How sure" uses the score bin, not the band. Every Unverified card in the prototype shows the same
  "16 of 35 confirmed" line, whether P_S is 0.22 or 0.65. Use calibration.parquet (test bins) instead, and say when
  the bin is tiny:
    P_S 0.49 to 0.61: "Of 7 listings we checked with a score like this, 5 were confirmed by a public source,
                       2 had no public information. (Small sample.)"
    P_S 0.18 to 0.28: "Of 8 listings we checked with a score like this, 1 was confirmed, 7 had no public
                       information."

Layout rule 4. Emergency plans (trauma, emergency C-section, burns) never lead with a [NO SERVICE TEXT] or
  [PLACE UNCLEAR] option. Such options go under "Other places nearby, not suitable on current evidence".

Layout rule 5. The call script is part of the card, because a phone call does not catch a wrong location when the
  number belongs to the real facility in another town (case C2):
    "Ask: 1) Do you do <service> now? 2) What is your full address and town? 3) Is someone available today?"

Layout rule 6. Distances above 100 km are given in hours ("about 4 to 7 hours"), never "400 to 641 min".

Layout rule 7. When a record has two candidate points, lead with the point that has more belief, and say which
  witness supports each point: "Probably in Sheikhpura (PIN 811101 and the listing's web pages say so). The map
  pin is near Sitamarhi, 55 km from you, but nothing else in the listing supports it."

Layout rule 8. The abstention line and P(at least one works) must not appear together as they do now
  ("ABSTAIN: No facility ... has enough public evidence" followed by "P(at least one of these works) = 0.88").
  Replace P(at least one works) on lay cards with "If the first call says no, call the next one." Keep the number
  only on the judge view, labelled "not yet checked against outcomes: no Witness Ledger top-1 answer has a label".


Case S1 (strong): Civil Hospital, Panipat, dialysis near Panipat district
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Now (prototype):
  [1] LIKELY offers dialysis - Civil Hospital, Panipat, Panipat
  [2] How sure it offers the service: 0.70 (range 0.38 to 0.97).
  [3] Why we think it offers it (sentences from its own listing): ... 3 quotes
  [4] Is it real: website none; phone none. P_E = 0.71 ...
  [8] Call first: 1802630102 (number found in the listing text, not checked)

Proposed level 0:
  [LIKELY, borderline] Civil Hospital, Panipat (government district hospital) - 9 km, about 13 to 21 min
  Call first: <number> (from the listing, not checked). Ask if dialysis runs today and the timing.
  Its listing says: "Hemodialysis facility run under Public-Private Partnership" (+2 similar)
  How sure: score 0.70, just at our 0.70 cut-off. We have not yet checked any listing this high.

Proposed level 1, new line under the quotes:
  "Most of this score comes from the kind of hospital it is: a government district hospital, where the public
   standard (IPHS 2022) expects dialysis (+24 points), with 20+ specialties (+9). The three sentences add
   8 points. Without them the score would be 0.64."
Fix the contradiction: [4] says "phone none" while [8] shows a number. phone_class and display_phones come from
  different tables; the existence head took a -0.7 logit penalty for "no phone" (-20 points) on a record that shows one.

Proposed level 2 (waterfall rows, values from recompute_s1.json, points = 20/ln 2 x contribution):
  base for dialysis (intercept x slope, plus offset)       -35.0
  text about dialysis: any own mention (t1)                 +8.3
  text: more units, more fields (t2, t4 with dialysis adj.)   0.0  (fitted to zero, see model card)
  related-service hints (p1, ICU or similar, net)          +10.0  <- quote the triggering sentence or tag
  merge-risk group "mid" (g_mid)                           +11.6  <- raises the score; say so
  district hospital (level_dh)                             +12.9
  IPHS says DH should offer it (iphs_essential)            +10.8
  20+ specialties, 100+ beds, 20+ doctors, public          +6.3
  total 24.8 points -> logit 0.859 -> P_S 0.702
  Column note: "fitted weight x calibration slope 0.38 = contribution". Without this note the weight column
  (t1 = 0.754) and the contribution column (0.286) do not match and a judge will think the numbers are wrong.


Case S2 (strong text, Unverified): Satyavrat Hospital, dialysis near Patna (q35)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Proposed level 0:
  [CALL FIRST] Satyavrat Hospital, Patna - 5 km, about 10 min
  Call first: <number>. Ask: dialysis now? full address? a slot this week?
  Its listing says: "Hemodialysis unit available" (+2 more, in 2 sections of the listing)
  Not checked by us. No test listing scored this high (test bins end at 0.61). In development data, 7 of 15
  listings scoring 0.48 to 0.75 were confirmed, 0 were wrong, 8 had no public information. (Small sample.)
Level 1 addition, the comparison a user will ask about: "Civil Hospital, Panipat is marked Likely and this one is
  not, though this listing has more dialysis text. The difference is the kind of hospital (district hospital,
  +24 points), not the text."
Level 1, near-tie notice: the 2nd and 3rd options changed when the query point moved 4.4 km (benchmark point vs
  resolver point), so print "options 1 to 3 are close; any of them is a reasonable first call" when U ratios are
  below 1.1.
Fix the echo: benchmark_answers says "Using Patna (district layer, median of 66 PIN points)" but the distances used
  the stored GPO PIN 800001 point, 4.4 km away. The echoed point must be the computed point.


Case U1 (uncertain): Referal Hospital, emergency C-section near Singwahini village, Sitamarhi (q01)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Now: the top option for a rural Bihar emergency. Card leads with "About 267 km by road (400 to 641 min)", then
  "Or 55 km if it is at its second recorded location (we put 64% of the location belief there)". No phone. No
  sentence about C-section. Backup 2 is "Dhanwantari Allergy & Asthma Clinic".
Proposed (this option moves under the fold per rule 4):
  [PLACE UNCLEAR] [NO SERVICE TEXT] Referal Hospital (listed city: Nalanda)
  Probably in Sheikhpura district, about 7 to 11 hours by road (267 km, rough): its PIN 811101 and a listing URL
  name Barbigha, Sheikhpura. The map pin is 55 km from you, but nothing else in the listing supports it.
  No sentence in its listing mentions C-section. No phone number in our data, so you cannot confirm before going.
  Not suitable on current evidence.
And the page-level line for this query:
  "We found no facility within 150 km whose listing says it does emergency C-sections and whose location we trust.
   Call 102/108 (state ambulance) or the nearest government hospital you know." (the helpline text is a product
   decision; it is not derived from the data)
Model fix behind the card: 1 - P_L is assigned to p2 by construction (spec 5.4). Here p2 is the raw coordinate
  point, whose own vote was -1, and the city vote is -1 only because Nalanda's centre is 29.5 km from p1 against a
  25 km cut-off, while loc_note says "'Nalanda' lies near the postcode". Require that p2 receive mass only from
  witnesses that support p2, or send the mass to "unknown".


Case U2 (uncertain): Smile Kraft Dental Clinic, trauma near Palamu (q12)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Now: the top option for trauma, 2 km. Card says "(no sentence of its own names the service: the score rests on
  facility type and size)".
That sentence is false for this card. The facility-type terms pull the score down (context -0.35 logit). The score
  rests on a prerequisite hit: "Provides 24 Hours Dental Emergency Services" was read as trauma.emergency_24x7
  (P = 1, +0.39 logit, about +11 points). The dentist penalty (-2.0 prior) never fired because the record's type
  is "clinic", although the name and all 10 specialties are dental.
Proposed:
  [NO SERVICE TEXT] Smile Kraft Dental Clinic, Palamu - 2 km
  "This is a dental clinic. Its listing does not mention trauma care. It appears only because it says
   '24 Hours Dental Emergency Services'. Not suitable for an injury."
Template fix: when T < 2, print the actual non-text drivers: the prerequisite sentence or tag that set P, and the
  top two context terms by points. Across the 40 benchmark queries, 113 cards have T < 2; 64 of them (0.57, Wilson
  0.47 to 0.65) have P >= 1, and 22 (0.19, 0.13 to 0.28) have a negative context total, so the current sentence is
  wrong or incomplete on most of them.


Case C1 (contaminated, caught): Wockhardt Hospital Nagpur, dialysis near Nagpur
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What works: rank 51 of 63 in Nagpur, 36 of 175 at Mumbai PIN 400078; the two Nair Hospital sentences are shown
  under "What we ignored" with the hospital named. Keep this section as it is; it is the clearest evidence of
  trust logic on any card.
Proposed when the record is shown (search, or "why not this one"):
  [PLACE UNCLEAR] Wockhardt Hospital Nagpur
  "Name and city say Nagpur. PIN 400078 and the map point say Mumbai (677 km apart). We put 58% on Mumbai.
   The landline starts with 22, the Mumbai code. Do not travel on this listing; call and ask for the address."
  "We ignored 2 dialysis sentences because they describe Nair Hospital, Mumbai Central."
Fixes: "890 km by road (1335 to 2136 min)" -> "about 22 to 36 hours"; the header "..., Nagpur" next to a Mumbai
  point must carry the warning; "In-house dialysis units" is quoted at claim idx 43 while the raw equipment array
  holds it at position 46 (blank items are skipped when indexing), so a "show in source" link would open the wrong
  item. 2,171 of 18,936 atoms (0.115, Wilson 0.110 to 0.119) have this offset; 18,934 of 18,936 texts do appear
  verbatim in the raw array. Store the raw position next to idx.


Case C2 (contaminated, not caught): Sharda Hospital (city "Greater Noida"), trauma near 25.15, 83.68 (q29)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Now: the top option, 60 km, P_L 0.77, "Agree: stated state. Point elsewhere: none." No warning. City "Greater
  Noida" with PIN 270001 (not in the India Post directory) and map point in Chandauli district. Landline starts with
  120 (Ghaziabad/Noida code; general knowledge, not checked here). Also rank 1 for a Chandauli trauma query.
  The cited "Pediatric trauma care." comes from a record whose source pages include a PubMed article.
Risk: the health worker calls "to confirm", reaches the real Sharda Hospital in Greater Noida, hears "yes, we do
  trauma", and sends the patient 60 km to a point where that hospital is not.
Proposed:
  [PLACE UNCLEAR] Sharda Hospital
  "Listed city Greater Noida is about 680 km (straight line) from the map point we have (Chandauli). The PIN is not a valid
   India Post PIN. On the call, ask for the full address before you send anyone."
Model fix: the query resolver places "Greater Noida" (city layer, median of 21 PIN points; 681 km from the map
  point), but the location features have d_city_km = null for this record, so the city vote abstained. Use the same
  place layers for votes and queries, and count a stated city that cannot be placed as a dissent (or at least block
  P_L >= 0.7), not as an abstention. 22 of 181 benchmark cards (0.12, Wilson 0.08 to 0.18) have an unresolved city whose vote silently
  abstained.
