# Hindi glossary

One Hindi term per concept, used in every user-facing string (tiles, cards, read-aloud, family card, slip,
pulse, field lines). Readers are ASHAs and community health workers in rural Bihar, UP, Jharkhand and MP, and
patients' families, often hearing the text through the phone's hi-IN voice. So: plain spoken Hindustani, common
English loanwords in Devanagari, short sentences, आप, digits for numbers, facility names in their own script.

Reviewed 2026-11-22 (see review/HINDI_REVIEW_2026-11-22.md). This is a desk review, not a field test.

## Bands and safety words

| English concept | Hindi term | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Likely (band) | संभावना ज़्यादा | Pairs with संभावना कम so a listener hears high vs low chance. The old "संभावना है" (there is a chance) undersold it, and "संभावित" (pulse) was a second, more formal word. |
| Not verified (band) | पक्का नहीं | "पक्का" is the everyday word for certain. Replaces "पुष्टि नहीं" (formal). Read aloud as "पक्का नहीं, पहले फ़ोन करके पूछें" to match the English "not verified, call first". |
| Unlikely (band) | संभावना कम | Unchanged. |
| Call first (to check) | पहले फ़ोन करके पूछें | Band line, field-report disagreement lines and the family card. One phrase for the instruction attached to an uncertain option. |
| Call before you go | जाने से पहले फ़ोन करें | Used for the "card or slip is old" lines and the call-ahead heading. Different concept from the band instruction. |
| Its listing says | इसकी लिस्टिंग में लिखा है | "Listing" means the facility's own written description. "सूची" was also used, but it reads as "a list of hospitals" and suggested an official register. |
| Listed for this service | लिस्टिंग में यह सेवा लिखी है | States what is written, not that the service is confirmed. |
| Not a medical opinion | यह डॉक्टर की सलाह नहीं है | Plain, and says who to ask instead. |
| Confirmed / confirmation | पक्का | "पक्की पुष्टि" was doubled and formal. |

## Places, people and things

| English concept | Hindi term | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Facility, hospital | अस्पताल | Used for every facility type. Families call a CHC or PHC "अस्पताल" too. |
| Emergency (department, situation) | इमरजेंसी | What people say and what hospital signs say. Replaces "आपात" and "आपात स्थिति". |
| 24x7 emergency | 24 घंटे खुली इमरजेंसी | The voice reads "24x7" as letters and digits. |
| Ambulance, free | एम्बुलेंस, मुफ़्त | Unchanged. |
| Pregnant woman or infant (102) | गर्भवती महिला या शिशु | 102 (JSSK) covers sick infants up to one year, so "नवजात शिशु" (newborn) was too narrow; "गर्भावस्था" (pregnancy) named a state, not a person. |
| Health worker | स्वास्थ्य कार्यकर्ता | Covers ASHA, ANM and other community workers. |
| Field report | स्वास्थ्य कार्यकर्ताओं की रिपोर्ट | Says who wrote it. "फ़ील्ड रिपोर्ट" is office jargon. |
| Report (field report, short form) | रिपोर्ट | Only where the health worker context is clear. A medical test report is always "जाँच रिपोर्ट". |
| Referral | रेफ़रल | Used in NHM training and on forms. |
| Referral slip | रेफ़रल पर्ची, short पर्ची | "पर्ची" is the everyday word for a paper slip. Replaces "स्लिप". |
| Doctor's note | डॉक्टर की पर्ची | Common phrase for a prescription or note. |
| Family journey card | यात्रा कार्ड | Kept from the card title. "परिवार कार्ड" was used in toasts, but it sounds like a ration card or family ID card. |
| Patient | मरीज़ | Unchanged. |
| Service | सेवा | Unchanged. |
| Treatment | इलाज | Only for real treatment. Not used with MRI or other tests. |
| Test, assessment | जाँच | |
| District | ज़िला | |
| Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY | आयुष्मान कार्ड | What families call it; the voice mangles "PM-JAY". |
| MCP card | जच्चा-बच्चा (MCP) कार्ड | The mother and child card a pregnant woman carries. "JSSK card" does not exist as a card. |
| Bed | बेड | Hospital staff and families say "बेड खाली नहीं". |
| Took the patient | मरीज़ को ले लिया / मरीज़ को लिया गया | |

## Spelling rules

- Nukta on फ़ and ज़ (फ़ोन, मुफ़्त, ज़िला, ज़्यादा, मरीज़). No nukta on क, ख, ग (खराब, गलत), as in common print.
- Chandrabindu where it is standard (जाएँ, हाँ, आँकड़े, जाँच).
- No slashes, "x" or Latin abbreviations in anything read aloud; use या, words, or the Devanagari loanword.
- Labels that go into "{label} के लिए" or "{label_hi} के ... रेफ़रल" must stay grammatical in the oblique case: a
  feminine head noun (दिल की सर्जरी, जलने की चोट) or an invariant one (डायलिसिस, MRI स्कैन).
