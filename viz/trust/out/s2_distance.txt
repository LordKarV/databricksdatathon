Of 9,869 records with coordinates and a postcode point, 1,367 (13.9%) place the map pin more than 50 km from the record's own postcode, and 105 more than 500 km. Records whose own text says the facility is located in another district or state are far from their postcode 52.9% of the time (723 of 1367), against 10.1% (821 of 8165) for all other records: the coordinates follow the other facility.
