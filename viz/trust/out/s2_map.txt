Point map of India without boundaries. 1,367 arrows, one per record whose listed coordinates sit more than 50 km from the centre of its own postcode, drawn from postcode to pin. 726 of them belong to records whose own text says the facility is located in another district or state, and 128 pins land in a different state from the postcode. Most arrows stay inside one region, but some cross the country: Gem Hospital, Bathinda (postcode in Punjab) is pinned about 2,350 km away, and the Aravind Eye Hospital record listed in Hyderabad has a Delhi postcode and a pin in Tamil Nadu, 1,871 km apart.
