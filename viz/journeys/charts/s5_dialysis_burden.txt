Left: dot map of 780 mainland district population centres with no boundaries, coloured by the population-weighted median weekly road km for three dialysis sessions at the nearest corroborated unit (408 units). Right: the 20 most burdened districts of at least 1 million people, led by Katni, Madhya Pradesh at 1,786 km a week. Nationally the population-weighted median is 384 km a week and 31% of people face 600 km or more. The facility records miss most of the 1,994 public dialysis centres the government counts in 751 districts, so this measures the evidence gap, not real access.
