Horizontal 100% stacked bars, one per pathway and origin set, showing the share of origins whose best plan is direct, a stabilising stop first, or abstain. Major trauma: stop first at 332 of 450 state-sample origins (74%, 95% CI 70 to 78%). Obstetric emergency: stop first at Benchmark (40 queries) 28%, Bihar 49%, Uttar Pradesh 31%, Maharashtra 38%. Heart attack (STEMI): no stop plans; direct for nearly all origins (19 of 487 planned origins are beyond the 120 min lysis gate, but only 48 records mention thrombolysis). Snakebite: abstain for every origin because only 5 records mention anti-snake venom. Probabilities are the derived/v1 stub.
