Two panels of the share of records where a claim sentence names another institution, by number of source URLs. Records citing research papers: 32.6% pooled vs 8.0% for other records; among records with 31 to 50 URLs 37.2% vs 22.5%. Records citing listing pages: 15.5% vs 3.9% pooled, but within size bands the gap is small until 31 to 50 URLs (26.7% vs 15.1%). Research-style claims are 0.84% of claims in research-citing records vs 0.02% elsewhere.
