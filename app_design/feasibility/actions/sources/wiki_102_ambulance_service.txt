The '''Free Ambulance Service''' is an [[Emergency medical services|emergency medical transport service]] in [[India]] that is usually reserved for newborns and pregnant women. It is also called National Ambulance Service (NAS). Under this service, all the ambulances are fitted with GPS system (for easy tracking) and other necessary medical equipment.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://archive.indianexpress.com/news/ambulances-waiting-in-wings-govt-proposes-another-service/975534/0|title=Ambulances waiting in wings, govt proposes another service|last=service|first=Express news|access-date=2016-07-01}}</ref> This emergency transport service facilitates 24x7 free of cost service to pregnant women, newborn babies and their mothers as well under the [[National Health Mission#Janani Suraksha Yojana|Janani SurakshaYojana]] and [[National Health Mission#Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)|Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/allahabad/Health-minister-flags-off-102-ambulance-service/articleshow/40617594.cms|title=Health minister flags off 102 ambulance service|newspaper=The Times of India|date=22 August 2014 |access-date=2016-07-01}}</ref> The ambulance service provides first aid to the patient and transport them to the nearest Community Health Centre or [[Public hospital|Government hospitals]]. There are 10017 ambulances operating as 102 patient transport. In some states, 5484 empanelled vehicles are also used as 102 ambulances to transport pregnant women and children to nearest healthcare facility.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=https://nhm.gov.in/index1.php?lang=1&level=2&sublinkid=1217&lid=189|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201022013057/https://nhm.gov.in/index1.php?lang=1&level=2&sublinkid=1217&lid=189|url-status=dead|archive-date=22 October 2020|title=ERS/PATIENT TRANSPORT SERVICE|website=www.nhm.gov.in|access-date=2020-03-18}}</ref>

== History ==
102 National Ambulance Service was started on 17 January 2014 in Uttar Pradesh.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://indianexpress.com/article/cities/lucknow/after-108-cm-flags-off-102-ambulance-service/|title=After 108, CM flags off 102 ambulance service|date=17 July 2014|work=Indian Express|access-date=1 July 2016}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.dailypioneer.com/state-editions/lucknow/akhilesh-yadav-flagged-off-300-air-conditioned-102-ambulances.html|title=Akhilesh Yadav flagged off 300 air-conditioned 102 Ambulances|website=www.dailypioneer.com|access-date=2016-07-01|archive-date=10 May 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170510110605/http://www.dailypioneer.com/state-editions/lucknow/akhilesh-yadav-flagged-off-300-air-conditioned-102-ambulances.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> A fleet of around 2270 [[Ambulance]]s is operational in all the districts of the State, to date. On an average, around 65,000 calls are received and 25,000 trips serviced on any working day in Uttar Pradesh. Around ten million women and newborns have benefited from this service, until 2016.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|url=http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/Over-1-5-cr-benefited-from-free-ambulance-services-in-UP/articleshow/52222940.cms|title='Over 1.5 cr benefited from free ambulance services in UP'|newspaper=The Times of India|date=11 May 2016 |access-date=2016-07-01}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=102 Service Delivery Data |url=http://upnrhm.gov.in/site-files/102_Service_Delivery_Data-March_2016.pdf |website=Uttar Pradesh National Health Mission |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160920022658/http://upnrhm.gov.in/site-files/102_Service_Delivery_Data-March_2016.pdf |archive-date=20 September 2016 |date=March 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref> This service is provided through a private service provider Ganapati Venkata Krishnanreddy Emergency Management and Research Institute GVK-EMRI which provides free ambulance services under government-run helplines in 17 states and union territories in [[India]].<ref name=":2" />

After the success of 102 Emergency Medical Ambulance Services (OEMAS) in Uttar Pradesh, many states also launched the 102 ambulance service for free referral transport of pregnant women and sick infants under Janani Sishu Surakhya Karyakram (JSSK) programme. Some of the states to launch 102 service include [[Odisha]], [[Madhya Pradesh]], [[Sikkim]], [[Jharkhand]], [[Himachal Pradesh]], [[West Bengal]] and [[Uttarakhand]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://odishachannel.com/index.php/786/102-ambulance-service-launched-in-odisha/|title= 102 ambulance service launched in Odisha|website=www.odishachannel.com|access-date=2014-07-11}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://nhm.assam.gov.in/schemes/national-ambulances-services-%E2%80%9C102%E2%80%9D|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171012005943/http://nhm.assam.gov.in/schemes/national-ambulances-services-%E2%80%9C102%E2%80%9D|url-status=dead|archive-date=12 October 2017|title=National Ambulances Services-"102"|website=www.nhm.assam.gov.in/|access-date=2020-03-18}}</ref><ref name=":1" /> In Odisha alone, the maternal mortality rate has gone 81 points down from 303 in 2006 to 222 in 2013 after the introduction of 102 ambulance service by the state government in private public partnership with Ziqitza Healthcare under the JSSK programme.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web|url=https://www.zhl.org.in/services/ambulance-services/|title=JANANI SHISHU SURAKSHA|website=www.zhl.org.in|access-date=2020-03-18}}{{Dead link|date=June 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref>

== Operation ==
It consists of an Emergency Response Centre (ERC) which is a centralized call centre that receives and handles the emergency phone calls. The call centre is staffed with Emergency Response Officers (ERO) by GVK-JSS in Aashiana, [[Lucknow]]. They take the emergency call, determine the location and send an ambulance to the place.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.igovernment.in/articles/35447/up-rolls-out-ambulance-service-for-villages|title=UP rolls out ambulance service for villages / igovernment.in|website=www.igovernment.in|access-date=2016-07-01}}</ref> That ambulance reaches the patient in 20 minutes in cities and around 30 minutes in rural places.<ref name=":0" />

Similarly, other states of India have their own centralized call centres to receive 102 emergency ambulance service calls and send the ambulances to the emergency spot. Some states use empanelled vehicles as 102 ambulance service namely Janani express in MP & Odisha, Mamta Vahan in Jharkhand, Nishchay Yan Prakalpa in West Bengal, and Khushiyo ki Sawari in Uttarakhand. All the state ambulances supported under [[National Health Mission]] (NHM) abide by the mandatory NAS guidelines.<ref name=":1" />
